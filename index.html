FAQ

Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about working with Zeezen.

What services does Zeezen offer?
Zeezen offers a full suite of digital marketing services including Social Media Marketing, Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Google Ads & Paid Advertising, Branding & Identity Design, Content Marketing Strategy, and Website Growth Optimization. We tailor each service to fit your business goals and budget.
How long before I see results?
Results vary by service. Paid advertising (Google Ads, social media ads) can show results within days to weeks. SEO and organic content typically take 3–6 months to gain momentum. We provide monthly reports so you can track progress every step of the way.
Do you work with small businesses or startups?
Absolutely. We work with businesses of all sizes — from early-stage startups to established companies. Our strategies are scaled to match your budget and growth stage, ensuring you get maximum impact whether you're just starting out or looking to expand.
How does the onboarding process work?
Our onboarding is fast and smooth — typically completed within 48 hours. After you reach out, we schedule a discovery call to understand your brand, goals, and challenges. We then build a custom strategy blueprint and kick off execution within the week.
What makes Zeezen different from other agencies?
We combine creative excellence with data-driven strategy — never one without the other. You get a dedicated team (not a revolving door of account managers), full transparency with live reporting dashboards, and strategies built specifically for your brand — not cookie-cutter templates.
How do I get started with Zeezen?
Simple — just fill out the contact form below or send us an email at zeezendigital.in@gmail.com. We'll respond within 24 hours to schedule a free strategy consultation. No commitment required, just a conversation about your goals.
Do you offer long-term contracts or flexible plans?
We offer both. You can start with a short-term project or a monthly retainer. While we recommend a minimum of 3 months to see meaningful results, we don't lock you into rigid long-term contracts. Our clients stay because of results, not paperwork.
